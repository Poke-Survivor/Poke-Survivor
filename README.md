# PokéSurvivor at SurvivorPoke

Free browser survival gameplay: choose a Pokémon partner, dodge enemy hordes, evolve moves and build your collection.

**[▶ Play at survivorpoke.com](https://survivorpoke.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=pokesurvivor&utm_content=profile_play)**

We maintain player resources for the version hosted at survivorpoke.com:

- [Game introduction and player documentation](https://github.com/Poke-Survivor/pokesurvivor)
- [Move evolutions and recipes](https://github.com/Poke-Survivor/pokesurvivor/blob/main/docs/move-evolutions.md)

- [Questions and reproducible player reports](https://github.com/Poke-Survivor/pokesurvivor/issues/new/choose)
